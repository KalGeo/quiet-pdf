# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

Quiet PDF Reader does not collect, store, share or transmit any personal data.

- **No internet access.** The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot
  send data anywhere.
- **No ads, analytics or trackers.** The app contains no advertising or analytics code.
- **No accounts.** There is nothing to sign in to.
- **Your files stay on your device.** A PDF is read only when you open it, and only on your
  device. The app can read only the files you choose yourself or open with it from another
  app; it has no general access to your storage.

## What the app saves on your device

To be useful between sessions, the app keeps a few things in its private storage on your
device:

- the list of recently opened files (their names and locations),
- the last page you read in each file,
- whether night mode is on.

When another app shares a PDF as a stream instead of a file, a temporary copy is kept in the
app's cache so it can be displayed.

None of this leaves your device, and it is excluded from cloud backup. It is deleted when
you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. You can remove individual files from the
recent list inside the app.
