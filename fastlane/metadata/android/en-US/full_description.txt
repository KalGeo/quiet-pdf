Quiet PDF Reader opens your PDFs and shows them to you. That is all it does.

There are no ads, no pop-ups, no accounts and no tracking. The app does not even have permission to use the internet, so nothing you read can leave your phone. It is small (around 1 MB) and open source.

<b>Reading</b>
• Smooth continuous scrolling through every page
• Pinch or double-tap to zoom; pages are redrawn sharp at any zoom level
• Full screen: the bars disappear as soon as you start reading, and a tap brings them back
• Night mode turns pages dark for comfortable reading in the evening

<b>Finding your way</b>
• Search for any word, with every match highlighted on the page (Android 15 and newer)
• Jump to any page by number or with a slider
• Reopens every document exactly where you stopped, however you open it next time
• A list of your recent files on the start screen

<b>Works with your other apps</b>
Open a PDF from your file manager, email, messages or downloads and choose Quiet PDF Reader.

<b>Privacy</b>
The app reads only the files you choose. It collects nothing, shares nothing and needs no permissions. Your recent files and reading positions are stored only on your phone.

<b>Open source</b>
The source code is public under the GPL-3.0 license:
https://github.com/KalGeo/quiet-pdf

Not supported: password-protected PDFs, text selection, annotations and forms.
